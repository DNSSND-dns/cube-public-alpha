01 / THE COMPLEX

About

Every door is
another question.

Enter a labyrinth of unusual rooms where familiar rules become uncertain. Explore, lose your sense of direction, and discover what happens when gravity stops being a constant.

Encounter other players and bots inside an atmosphere of quiet uncertainty. Observe your surroundings. There is more to a room than its walls.

This is a public alpha. Expect rough edges, strange behaviour, and a world still taking shape. Your feedback helps shape what comes next.
